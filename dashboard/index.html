The mined model

The model pmx discover actually mined — a real Petri net or BPMN, gateways and all. Paste the SVG it renders and it lands here, recoloured for whichever theme you are reading in.

No model pasted yet. Render one, then copy the file in:

pmx discover YOUR_LOG.xes --notation bpmn -o model.bpmn \
  -i model.svg --bgcolor transparent
pbcopy < model.svg

--bgcolor transparent is what lets it sit on this page in either theme. Needs Graphviz.

Getting your own data in

This page reads the exact JSON that pmx inspect emits — no conversion step. -n 25 controls how many paths travel with it.

pmx inspect examples/data/receipt.xes --json -n 25 | pbcopy

Then hit Load your own above and paste. Nothing leaves the page — it runs entirely in your browser, so a log you cannot upload anywhere is still fine to look at here.

Coverage figures count only the paths present in the JSON. With -n 25 on a log of 116 paths, the remaining 91 are real cases that simply are not itemised — which is why the meter below the fold never claims to reach 100%.
